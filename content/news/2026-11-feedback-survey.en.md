Kanarche is a non-commercial open-source project. We want it to be useful to the people who use it. To decide what to improve next, we need your opinion. So we have opened a short survey.

![The start page of the survey "Tell us how Kanarche works for you"](/static/news/feedback-survey-en.webp)

## Where to find the survey

The Feedback link is in the top bar of every page on the site. On a narrow screen, such as a phone, it is in the language menu. The survey is also on the [About](/en/about) page. You can open it directly at [feedback.kanarche.eu](https://feedback.kanarche.eu/?lang=en).

![The top bar of the site with the Feedback link](/static/news/feedback-link-en.webp)

## What we ask

The survey takes about 2 minutes. Every question is optional. There are six questions:

- How easy Kanarche is to use.
- How useful the information is.
- How fast the site works.
- What you use it for. For example the air near your home, a walk or sport outdoors, children or health, comparing areas, the sea and bathing water, pollen.
- What works well for you and what does not. You can write freely here.
- Contact, only if you want a reply.

The free text question is the place for ideas and for problems. If something is missing from the map, write it there. If something does not load, shows odd values or is slow, write that too. The more concretely you describe a problem, the easier it is for us to find it. It helps to mention the page, the area and the device you use.

## What happens to the answers

We do not ask for an email address. The contact field is only for people who want a reply. The survey runs on a server in the European Union. We store no IP address. The subdomain uses only session cookies the form needs to work. Only we can see the answers. There is more in the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy).

Later we may publish a summary article with the overall findings.

## Other ways to reach us

The Kanarche code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/iliyan-s-petkov/kanarche). If you have an account there, you can open an [issue](https://github.com/iliyan-s-petkov/kanarche/issues) for a bug or a suggestion. Anyone can see there what has already been reported.

Thank you to everyone who takes two minutes.
