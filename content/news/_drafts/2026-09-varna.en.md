Air in Varna was clean through September. The monthly mean of PM2.5 is 3.2 µg/m³ and of PM10 is 6.2 µg/m³.

| Measure | Monthly mean | Highest six hour mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PM2.5 | 3.2 µg/m³ | 10.4 µg/m³ |
| PM10 | 6.2 µg/m³ | 17.0 µg/m³ |

Both peaks fall in the night into 4 September. No six hour mean of PM10 goes above 50 µg/m³, the EU daily limit.

The numbers are for the Varna area and rest on 121 six hour points for the month. The data comes from the sensors on the map.
